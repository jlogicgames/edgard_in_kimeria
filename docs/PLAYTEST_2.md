# Second external playtest: feedback, analysis and decisions

**Status: done.** The second external playtest has run and its feedback reached the author on 2026-11-21. This document records that feedback, its analysis against the current build, the author's decisions of the same day and the GitHub issue that tracks each change. Everything it started is tracked under the umbrella issue [#563](https://github.com/jlogicgames/edgard_in_kimeria/issues/563); it also records which open check boxes the author accepted on the strength of this round (see [what this playtest verified](#what-this-playtest-verified)).

## Session

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Build | Web build (GitHub Pages), desktop browser |
| Participants | Players unfamiliar with the game, Ukrainian speakers (number not recorded) |
| Content | The build of the time: `forest-0` tutorial and the following forest levels; whether the ending was reached and how the audio sounded were not recorded |
| Form | Written feedback collected by the author and passed on as a list |
| Received | 2026-11-21 |

These are observations of other people, kept separate from the author's own play-throughs (ROADMAP, D33) and from headless replay checks (`RoomReplayTest`).

## What this playtest did not measure

The feedback is qualitative. No room times, death counts or retries were recorded. So:

- **No measured values.** The D19 hypotheses (20 s mastered, 60 s first learning) and the trap warning times stay as they are; no tester reported a problem with them, which is not a measurement.
- **Not covered:** the desktop build, touch devices, the German, Polish and Belarusian texts, the audio (the sound audition issues [#520](https://github.com/jlogicgames/edgard_in_kimeria/issues/520) and [#494](https://github.com/jlogicgames/edgard_in_kimeria/issues/494) stay open), the ending page, and the Steppe and Fortress levels (not built).
- **One language.** The players read Ukrainian, so the text-fit and wording remarks (F3, F5, F8) concern the Ukrainian catalog; native-speaker review is [#369](https://github.com/jlogicgames/edgard_in_kimeria/issues/369) and the shared wording checks are [#143](https://github.com/jlogicgames/edgard_in_kimeria/issues/143).
- **Environment and exit readability** ([#142](https://github.com/jlogicgames/edgard_in_kimeria/issues/142), [#128](https://github.com/jlogicgames/edgard_in_kimeria/issues/128)) still need a player who is watched, not only written remarks; F7, F9, F13, F17, F21 and F23 are evidence for them, not a pass.

## Patterns

- **Readability of the world again** (F9, F13, F16, F17, F19, F21, F23, F26 to F28): background and traps are still hard to tell from active objects, and exits and trap shapes do not say what they are. The author answered with a rule change (a clear, opaque decor tier in another palette, D76), new looks and redraws, not new mechanics.
- **Controls should be one button** (F2, F4, F12, F15): the chords, the wall jump and the hidden left-handed layout all asked the player to learn more than the room asks. The author removed the chord (Up and Down interact) and the wall jump; showing both hand layouts in Options is a proposal.
- **The tale voice is not finished** (F3, F5, F6, F8, F10, F11, F29, F30): the intro, the healer lines and the screens around them still read plainer than the tutorial tales; the author decided the Accessibility label, the skip hint and the named boss; the text rewrites, the book frame and the fairy on the dialogue screen are proposals.
- **Feedback on feel** (F23, F24, F25, F29): the blast, the coin and the pickup lack the small motions that carry weight; all are cosmetic and leave room state alone.
