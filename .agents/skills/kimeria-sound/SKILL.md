# Kimeria sound

A thin procedure. The rules and the recipe live in [SOUND_DIRECTION.md](../../../docs/SOUND_DIRECTION.md); read it, do not
restate it here. Work from the repository root; links resolve relative to this file.

## Read first

- [SOUND_DIRECTION.md](../../../docs/SOUND_DIRECTION.md): the aesthetic (crude, buzzy, 90s formant voice-synth; roughness is
  the goal), the recipe, "Making a new sound", the audition process and the "Don't" list.
- [STYLE_SYSTEM.md](../../../docs/STYLE_SYSTEM.md): a sound is the audible half of D75's "a hazard warns before it acts and
  shows a consequence after"; the falling stone (D73) is the model, the audit is
  [#388](https://github.com/jlogicgames/edgard_in_kimeria/issues/388). The visible half is
  [kimeria-particles](../kimeria-particles/SKILL.md).

## Steps

1. **Decide what the sound tells.** A warning (before a trap acts, D7: it must not be masked) or a consequence (after).
   Name the event and where it plays.
2. **Generate, never sample.** Add a function with named constants and a fixed seed to `tools/audio/generate_sounds.py`
   and a row to `GAME_SOUNDS`; standard library only. Regenerate and confirm every other file is byte-identical
   (md5 before and after); never touch another sound's constants or seed.
3. **Check the numbers.** Add the bounds row to `tools/test_generate_sounds.py`; run
   `python3 -m unittest discover -s tools -p 'test_*.py'` and `python3 tools/audio/analyze_sounds.py --png art/audio/previews FILE`.
   The numbers check format, level, clicks and spectral shape; they cannot judge the sound.
4. **Wire it.** Add the name to `Assets.SOUND_EFFECTS` (`SoundPreloadTest` fails otherwise) and play it through
   `KimeriaGame.playSound(...)` behind `playSounds()`. No allocation in the per-frame caller (AGENTS.md).
5. **Audition.** The author listens; the agent cannot. Offer the default plus at most one alternative (kept outside
   `assets/`), say what to hear and where in the game, and delete the loser after the choice.
6. **Record.** The sound's section in SOUND_DIRECTION.md (seed, constants, verdict), the preview PNG, and anything not
   heard yet as "not verified" with an open issue (AGENTS.md).
